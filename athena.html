Athena

Ἀθηνᾶ
Roman: Minerva

Athena: gray-eyed goddess of wisdom, strategy, and crafts. Born from the head of Zeus, and patron goddess of Athens, heroes, and leaders.

I have always had an affinity for Athena. I have always admired the qualities she embodies, and tried to magnify them in myself. I try to be a strategic thinker, to plan ahead, always have a backup plan, and make wise decisions. In this way, I have dedicated my life to Athena, and the ideals she represents from before I knew her name.

Pallas Athena, Grey-eyed Athena, I call upon thee.
Grant me wisdom. Grant me foresight.
Grant me the protection of your spear and aegis.
