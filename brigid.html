Brigid: triple goddess of the fire of the forge, healing, and poetry. Her name means “The Exalted One”.

I learned about Brigid on my travels to Ireland in 2025. Since then, she has been a major influence. My affinity is through writing which includes poetry, and making things, as one does on the forge. I am less of a healer, at least in the physical sense, but I do try. That journey to Ireland was transformative, and Brigid is the embodiment of that. With family ties to Ireland, the Emerald Isle calls to me still.

Lady of the Sacred Flame, Mistress of the Mantle, Brigid, Flame of Inspiration, I call upon thee.
Grant me your inspiration. Grant me your healing.
Grant me your beauty and your dedication.
