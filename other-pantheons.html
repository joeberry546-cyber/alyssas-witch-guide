Basic entries for five pantheons. Tap a group, then a god.

About these entries ▾

These are basics, not the full treatment the Greek, Norse and Celtic pages get. Each pantheon has a handful of its best-known figures, with domains, symbols, animals and family where they are well known. Names and spellings vary a lot between languages and eras, so the most common English form comes first, with another name beside it where useful.

Living traditions ▾

The Hindu gods are worshipped today by hundreds of millions of people, and the Maya and Nahua peoples of Mesoamerica are still here, so some of this is a living religion and not only a mythology. The Hindu entries are short, and one deity can be seen as an aspect of another. A page like this can only skim the surface, so treat it as a starting point and not a substitute for learning from people who practice.

A note on festivals ▾

Most of these festivals follow lunar or local calendars, so their dates shift from year to year. I give approximate seasons, and exact dates only where they are fixed. Aztec festival months come from 16th-century Spanish chroniclers and are approximate. Where a deity has no widely known festival, the Festivals line is simply left out.
