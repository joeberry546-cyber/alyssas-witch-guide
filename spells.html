Words I’ve written to work with

daily spell

armor around me, and armor within unbending, unbroken through chaos and din anchored in wisdom and rooted in place so only good fortune shall enter my space

ritual

I cast this circle round and round, a sacred space on holy ground from east to south, from west to north, protection, power, now come forth. Between the worlds this space shall be, a place of sacred mystery. By Earth and Air, by Fire and Sea, As I will, so mote it be.

facing east

Guardians of the East, Powers of Air, I call upon your wisdom, clarity and inspiration. Stand with me within this sacred circle. Hail and welcome.

facing south

Guardians of the South, Powers of Fire, I call upon your passion, energy, and transformation. Stand with me within this sacred circle. Hail and welcome.

facing west

Guardians of the West, Powers of Water, I call upon your healing, intuition, and cleansing flow. Stand with me within this sacred circle. Hail and welcome.

facing north

Guardians of the North, Powers of Earth, I call upon your strength, stability, and protection. Stand with me within this sacred circle. Hail and welcome.

the center

Earth below me, Air around me, Fire within me, Water flowing through me, Spirit surrounding me. The circle is cast. The quarters are called. The sacred space is prepared. So mote it be.

facing north

Guardians of the North, Powers of Earth, thank you for your presence. Go if you must, stay if you will, with gratitude and peace. Hail and farewell.

facing west

Guardians of the West, Powers of Water, thank you for your presence. Go if you must, stay if you will, with gratitude and peace. Hail and farewell.

facing south

Guardians of the South, Powers of Fire, thank you for your presence. Go if you must, stay if you will, with gratitude and peace. Hail and farewell.

facing east

Guardians of the East, Powers of Air, thank you for your presence. Go if you must, stay if you will, with gratitude and peace. Hail and farewell.

chant

The work is finished, the rite is done, Moon and stars, Earth and Sun. The powers gathered now depart With gratitude within my heart. The sacred boundary I release, May all return in perfect peace. The circle opens, yet love remains, Balance restored to earthly plains. So mote it be.
